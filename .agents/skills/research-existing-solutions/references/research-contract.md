# Research output contract

Include:

1. Decision and scope.
2. Search date, queries, and source-selection rules.
3. Project constraints and comparison dimensions.
4. Candidate table with direct primary-source links.
5. License and provenance notes.
6. Evidence-backed findings.
7. Build, adopt, adapt, and defer options.
8. Recommendation with tradeoffs and confidence.
9. Unknowns, drift-prone facts, and spike or verification plan.
10. Trigger, stop condition, and one explicit `build`, `adopt`, `adapt`, or `defer` disposition.
11. Conditions that require reopening the decision during implementation or repair.
12. For a reopened trigger, the prior assessment ID and whether new evidence supersedes a blocked
    assessment without deleting it.

Prefer primary sources. For technical claims, use official documentation, standards, canonical repositories, or original papers. Label community commentary as secondary evidence.
